Here is a repository that has a bunch of advice and resources: github.com/shaily99/advice#sop. If the amount of links there is overwhelming (they are to me!), here is some more distilled advice I've collected:

From the University of Toronto Graduate Application Assistance Program slides:
The goal of the statement of purpose is to explain your background, research interests, goals, and reasons for wanting a specific program. You should show:

  • Understanding of what research is
  • Clear interests (ok if broad)
  • How you have used your opportunities
  • Meaningful motivation for the problems you want to tackle
  • Knowledge of the specific program & your desired supervisors
  • Clear, correct, articulate writing; neat formatting
  • Do you have undergrad/prev research experience? What was your role?
  • Do you have publications? What was your role? What kind of venue?
  • Have you participated in contests? Conducted independent projects? Won prestigious awards?
  • Anything else that suggests brilliance, initiative, knowledge, dedication, motivation, drive...

Recommended Format for a SoP

  1. Introduction
    • The most important paragraph! Clearly and concisely convey what research questions you are interested in studying and why the problems you want to tackle are important.
  2. Research Experiences
    • For each experience you have, state your personal contributions to the project and what you learned.
  3. Presentations / Service / Awards / Honors (can split these into separate paragraphs as needed)
    • This will help convince the professors that you will make a positive impact on the research community, both socially and technically!
  4. Specific School Paragraph
    • Who do you want to work with at this university and why?
    • Why this university?
    • The goal is to convince the reader (a) why this program is the best fit for you, and (b) why you are the best fit for this program. Keep both in mind!
  5. Future Directions
    • What do you want to accomplish after your PhD program (in your career)? And how will this grad program help you achieve that goal? (It's totally okay to not have this fully mapped out yet—just include a sentence or two about whether you want to continue researching this topic in your career, and how this program will equip you with the skills to do so!)

Good luck with your application! If you do go meandering through the Notion site of example SoPs, I would encourage you to stay positive, think about what you can take away from each statement you read, and not get dragged into negative thinking of comparing yourself to others. Your unique profile is what matters!
