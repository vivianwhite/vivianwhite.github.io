How did computer vision evolve from recognizing objects in images to today's powerful AI systems? In this session for Cohere Labs ML Summer School 2026, Yash Mali and I trace the development of visual intelligence, covering foundational vision techniques, the deep learning revolution, modern transformer-based architectures, and the emergence of vision-language models. Viewers will gain insight into major breakthroughs that have shaped the field, current limitations of state-of-the-art models, and exciting open research questions that continue to drive advances in visual AI. The session concludes with a discussion of future directions and challenges, including reasoning, robustness, interpretability, and the development of more capable and trustworthy AI systems.
